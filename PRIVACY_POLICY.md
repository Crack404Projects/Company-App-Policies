# Privacy Policy for QR Code Catalyst

**Effective Date:** July 31, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how **QR Code Catalyst** ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
**QR Code Catalyst is designed as an offline-first application.**
* **Scanned & Generated Data:** All QR codes and barcodes you scan or generate (including URLs, contact cards, Wi-Fi networks, and calendar events) are stored **locally on your device storage**. We do not transmit, collect, or store your scan history on any external servers.
* **Camera Access:** We require access to your device's camera strictly to scan QR codes and barcodes in real time. We do not record video, capture photos in the background, or save camera footage.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services & Advertising
While our core scanning features work offline, we use **Google AdMob** to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may automatically collect certain device information to serve relevant ads, including:
* Advertising ID (Android/iOS)
* IP Address & General Location
* Device performance and crash logs

For more information on how Google AdMob uses your data, please review the [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

### 3. External Web Links
Our app allows you to scan QR codes that may open URLs in your browser. We are not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of any third-party websites you choose to visit.

---

### 4. Your Control Over Data
* **Clearing History:** You can permanently delete all locally stored scan history at any time by going to **Settings > Clear App History**.
* **Camera Permissions:** You can revoke camera access at any time through your device's system settings.

---

### 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.
