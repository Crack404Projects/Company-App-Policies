# Privacy Policy for QR Code Catalyst

**Effective Date:** July 31, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how **Crack404** ("we," "our," or "us"), the developer of the mobile application **QR Code Catalyst**, collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our app.

---

## 1. Information We Collect and Use

QR Code Catalyst is designed as an offline-first application. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personal information on external servers.

*   **Scanned & Generated Data:** All QR codes and barcodes you scan or generate (including URLs, contact cards, Wi-Fi networks, text, and calendar events) are stored locally on your device storage. We do not transmit, collect, share, or store your scan history on any external servers.
*   **Camera Access (`CAMERA`):** We require access to your device's camera strictly to scan QR codes and barcodes in real time. We do not record video, capture background photos, or transmit camera footage to any server.
*   **Flashlight & Hardware Control (If Applicable):** We may access your device's flashlight/torch solely to assist with scanning QR codes in low-light environments.
*   **Storage / Media Access (If Applicable):** If you choose to save a generated QR code image to your gallery or scan a QR code from a saved image, the app accesses your local device storage strictly to complete that action.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

While our core scanning features work offline, we use **Google AdMob** to display advertisements within the app. Google AdMob may automatically collect certain device information to serve relevant or non-personalized ads, including:

*   Advertising ID (Android/iOS)
*   IP Address & General Location (derived from IP address)
*   Device performance, diagnostics, and crash logs

We do not combine this third-party advertising data with your locally stored scan history. For more information on how Google AdMob collects and processes your data, please review the [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 3. Children's Privacy

QR Code Catalyst is a general-audience application and does not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through third-party ad integrations, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

---

## 4. External Web Links

Our app allows you to scan QR codes that may open URLs in your device's web browser. We are not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of any third-party websites or services you choose to visit.

---

## 5. Your Control Over Your Data

*   **Clearing History:** You can permanently delete all locally stored scan and generation history at any time by navigating to **Settings > Clear App History** within the app.
*   **Revoking Permissions:** You can disable Camera, Flashlight, or Storage permissions at any time through your device's system settings (**Settings > Apps > QR Code Catalyst > Permissions**).
*   **Ad Tracking Preferences:** You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID in your device's privacy settings to control interest-based advertising.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because QR Code Catalyst does not transmit your personal data or scan history to our servers, **Crack404** does not retain any user data on our end. Deleting the app or clearing your app data from your device permanently removes all locally stored QR code history.

---

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
