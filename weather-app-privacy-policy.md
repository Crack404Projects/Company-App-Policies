**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Weather**, developed by **Crack404**. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the data you share with us while using our weather mobile application (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains what information our App collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding your data.

---

## 2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

### A. Location Data (Precise and Approximate)
* **What We Collect:** With your permission, the App accesses your device's precise (GPS) or approximate location.
* **Why We Collect It:** We use this data strictly to provide real-time local weather conditions, multi-day forecasts, and severe weather notifications tailored to your current area.
* **Data Handling:** Your location coordinates are sent securely to our weather data provider (**OpenWeatherMap**) to retrieve weather metrics. We do not store, track, or share your location history on any external developer servers.

### B. Locally Stored Data (On-Device)
* **What We Store:** Bookmarked favorite cities, cached weather forecasts, and app preference settings.
* **How It Is Stored:** All saved locations and cached data are stored locally on your device using **Hive** (local database). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.
* **How to Delete:** You can clear this data at any time by removing saved cities within the App, clearing the App's storage data in your device settings, or uninstalling the App.

### C. Advertising Data
* **What We Collect:** Our App integrates advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), IP addresses, and general usage metrics.
* **Why We Collect It:** This information is used by Google AdMob to serve relevant banner and interstitial advertisements and to measure ad performance.

## 3. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. However, the App relies on select third-party services to function and monetize, which may collect data under their own privacy policies:

* **OpenWeatherMap API:** Used to fetch weather data based on geographic coordinates or city search queries.
    * [OpenWeatherMap Privacy Policy](https://openweathermap.org/privacy-policy)
* **Google AdMob & Google Play Services:** Used for ad delivery, analytics, and core app functionality.
    * [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
    * [How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## 4. Notifications

With your consent, the App generates local notifications to alert you to severe weather updates and daily forecasts. These notifications are scheduled and triggered locally on your device; no external push notification servers track your device for alert delivery.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable legal age in your region). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data without consent, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

---

## 6. Security

We prioritize the security of your information by utilizing secure network protocols (HTTPS) when transmitting location coordinates to our weather provider. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

---

## 7. Your Privacy Rights and Controls

Depending on your operating system, you have the following controls:
* **Location Permissions:** You can enable or disable location access at any time through your device's **Settings > Apps > Weather > Permissions**. (Note: Disabling location access will require you to search for cities manually).
* **Ad Personalization:** You can opt out of personalized advertising by navigating to your device's Google/Apple privacy settings and resetting or disabling your Advertising ID.
* **Data Deletion:** Uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally stored favorites and cache data from your device.
